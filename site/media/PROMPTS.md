# How the three images were made

Model: `google/nano-banana-2` on Replicate, `aspect_ratio` 16:9, `resolution` 1K, jpg. One call each.
The same scene was also run through `black-forest-labs/flux-2-pro`; the Nano Banana renders were chosen.

Shared style suffix on every prompt:

> Cinematic photograph, near-black background with a faint dot grid, dramatic low key lighting,
> deep red #e2342b accents as the only colour, thin glowing red network lines and small red nodes,
> shallow depth of field, premium editorial look. No text, no letters, no logos, no watermark.
> Keep the bottom fifth of the frame dark and calm.

- `hero-kitchen.jpg` — A small-business owner's open laptop on a kitchen table at night in a Queensland
  home, a terminal window glowing softly, a cardboard box of products and a phone beside it, a second
  chair pulled up close.
- `two-hands.jpg` — Two pairs of hands near one laptop keyboard: the owner's hands resting on the keys,
  a mentor's hand pointing at the screen, a delivery van key and a paper parcel on the table, glowing
  red thread lines running from the screen out into the dark.
- `shop-from-laptop.jpg` — A tiny glowing storefront built out of red light lines rising from a laptop
  screen, a map of suburban streets drawn in faint red lines across the dark table, a parcel and a cup
  of tea.
