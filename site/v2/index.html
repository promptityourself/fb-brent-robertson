The five steps

  1. 1

    Open the store the cheap way

    Basic plan, Shopify Payments, a free theme, your own domain connected. No paid apps or paid theme before the first sale.

    Australian pricing on 17 Oct 2026: Basic $56 a month, or $42 a month paid yearly. Online card rate 1.7% + 30¢ with Shopify Payments. 3 days free, then $1 a month for 3 months.

    Shopify pricing, Australia · Free themes

  2. 2

    Products that sell on a phone

    Three or more photos per product, a description that says who it is for and why, a price, stock on hand. Collections keep the menu short.

    Build it on the laptop, check every page on the phone. Shopify's own setup guide: edit the theme's sections, set up menus, and link the store policies from them.

    Setting up your online store

  3. 3

    Delivery area, pickup, GST

    Local delivery from your location, by radius or postcode list. Minimum order, delivery fee, free delivery over an amount. Local pickup on as well.

    Shopify allows up to 10 delivery zones per location, a radius up to 160 km, and up to 3 price-based rules per zone. North Lakes is postcode 4509; a postcode list or a 15 km radius both work.

    Local delivery · Taxes in Australia

  4. 4

    Checkout, policies, test orders

    Shopify Payments for cards and wallets. Refund, shipping, terms and contact pages generated in Settings > Policies. Test before the password comes off.

    Shopify's advice: place at least one test order during setup and after any payment change. Test mode, then a real order on your own card that you refund.

    Store policies · Test orders

  5. 5

    Social and email on your own name

    The free Facebook & Instagram sales channel, so people buy from your posts. Business email on your domain, because Shopify does not host mailboxes.

    The channel needs a Facebook Page owned by your business portfolio and a store that is no longer password protected. Shopify names Google Workspace and Zoho Mail for email on a custom domain.

    Email hosting

Nobody on the show has built a Shopify store yet. Brent would be the first. Every step above is Shopify's own documented setup, linked from the step.
